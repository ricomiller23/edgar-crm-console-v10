SEC Filings, Rule 144 & Securities Intelligence • V10 Release

EDGAR CRM Console V10

Enterprise relationship management console marrying SEC EDGAR institutional filings with pipeline tracking, advisor notes, and deal-stage progression.

V10 Module #1

Live SEC EDGAR Filing Sync to Contact & Account Timelines

Capability: Automatically attaches every new 8-K, 10-Q, 10-K, or Form 4 to the associated corporate account in the CRM without manual data entry.

Rationale: Ensures dealmakers have an instantaneous, comprehensive record of all official regulatory events when engaging in executive conversations.

V10 Module #2

Custom Deal Stage & Pipeline Revenue Forecasting

Capability: Introduces configurable Kanban pipeline stages (Sourced, Initial Audit, NDA Signed, Term Sheet Out, In Drafting, Closed) with weighted success probability forecasting.

Rationale: Transforms raw regulatory scouting into an institutional revenue pipeline with clear visibility into quarterly advisory fees.

V10 Module #3

Collaborative Internal Deal Notes & Secure Document Storage

Capability: Enables multi-user threaded commenting, @mentions, and encrypted PDF attachment uploads for term sheets, fee agreements, and clean cap tables.

Rationale: Centralizes advisory deal intelligence, eliminating fragmented communications across disparate email chains and local hard drives.

V10 Module #4

Automated Conflict of Interest & Cross-Portfolio Checker

Capability: Cross-references prospective advisory targets against existing client rosters, creditor lists, and board affiliations to flag conflicts before engagement letters are executed.

Rationale: Mitigates legal liability and compliance violations under FINRA and state legal ethics rules regarding dual representation.

V10 Module #5

Bulk CSV/Excel Export & Custom Advisory Metric Reporting

Capability: Provides granular reporting dashboards tracking outreach-to-deal conversion rates, average transaction velocity, and advisory fee yields by sector.

Rationale: Supplies managing partners with operational analytics needed to allocate advisory personnel and marketing spend efficiently.
